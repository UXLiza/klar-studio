We keep promises

KLAR STUDIO was founded in Zürich in 2015 by two architects who were tired of chaotic renovations and surprise costs. We only renovate apartments – nothing else. This focus lets us know every detail of Zürich’s old buildings and current building regulations inside out. All craftsmen are employed directly by us. No subcontractors, no excuses.

We believe a good renovation must be simple for the client: one contact person, one fixed price, one binding timeline. From the first visit to the final cleaning, everything runs on a clear plan. Over 180 apartments renovated, 98 % handed over on the agreed date, zero disputes about extra costs.
